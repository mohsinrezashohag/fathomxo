COPY STYLE RULES

The website copy should follow these rules:

Keep sentences short.

Use lowercase editorial headings where appropriate.

Use bold typography for important words.

Avoid corporate buzzwords like “innovative solutions”, “digital transformation”, “cutting-edge excellence”, etc.

Sound confident without sounding arrogant.

Keep the slightly rebellious personality of the reference site.

Use occasional casual language, but don't overdo profanity.

Make FathomXO feel like a software/product studio, not a traditional advertising agency.

Pricing should remain transparent and easy to understand.

Do not claim services, clients, technologies, or results that FathomXO does not actually provide.

All pricing should use ৳ BDT.

Make pricing clearly configurable from a single data structure so prices/features can easily be changed later.
