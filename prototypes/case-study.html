Case study / E-commerce

Rongdhonu sells three times faster than its old storefront.

Client
Rongdhonu
Year
2026
Service line
E-commerce
Duration
7 weeks

The problem

Rongdhonu ran a hosted storefront that took eleven seconds to load on the mid-range Androids most of their customers use. Stock counts lived in a spreadsheet that someone reconciled by hand every evening, so oversells were routine and refunds ate the margin on their best-selling lines.

The approach

We rebuilt the storefront headless, moved the catalogue behind a typed content layer, and wired inventory to their existing warehouse system so counts update on every order rather than once a day. The checkout was cut from five steps to two.

Everything shipped behind a first-load budget. A page that broke it did not go out, which meant saying no to two libraries the old site depended on.

  • Next.js
  • TypeScript
  • Postgres
  • Stripe
  • Vercel

The outcome

1.4sLargest contentful paint on 4G, from 11s
3.1×Conversion rate against the old storefront
0Oversells since inventory sync went live

They argued with us about two features and were right about both. That is the part we could not get from anyone else.

Nusrat Jahan, Founder, Rongdhonu
